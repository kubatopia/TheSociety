Dr. Z and the Riff Raff will perform for Sounds on the Square. The music is free; food and drink will be sold. Admission to the museum will be half price. Tables and chairs will be set up for those who want to sit on the courtyard; or bring your own chairs or blankets to sit on the lawn. Socializing begins at 6 p.m., and the music begin at 7.
