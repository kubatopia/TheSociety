Graves Anthony and Hunter Haskins will present “Patrick & Henry Distilled: A Look at the History of Legal Distilling at Patrick and Henry Counties" at 3 p.m. Sunday, Sept. 21, as part of the MHC Historical Society’s Sunday Afternoon Lecture Series. The program will be held at the MHC Heritage Museum, 1 E. Main St., Martinsville.
