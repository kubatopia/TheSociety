Celebrate the nation’s 250th anniversary of independence with a special patriotic-themed Sounds on the Square! Da Capo will perform music. Food will be for sale, along with beer and wine from Scuffle Hill Brewing. Admission to the event on the museum’s courtyard is free, and admission to the museum will be half-price until 9 p.m.
