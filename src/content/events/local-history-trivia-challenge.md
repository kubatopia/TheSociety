How much do you really know about our local area? Is it enough to win the Local History Trivia Challenge in honor of Local History Month in Virginia?

Join the Martinsville-Henry County Historical Society at Scuffle Hill Brewing Company on Wednesday, October 25, 2023, at 7:00 P.M., for an evening of facts, friendship, and fun. Joshua Duncan, Historical Society Board Member and local history teacher, will serve as host and emcee. The winning team will receive cool prizes - but most importantly, bragging rights!

The mission of the Martinsville-Henry County Historical Society is to promote an interest in the broad spectrum of history with emphasis on that of Martinsville and Henry County; to collect, preserve, interpret, and make available material relating to that history; to sponsor research and publications in the field of regional history; and to provide educational services to people and institutions through-out the area.
