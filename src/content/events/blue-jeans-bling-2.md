The MHC Historical Society’s annual music, dinner and social event - the Blue Jeans & Bling fundraiser–will be held on Saturday, Oct. 3! The social will begin at 6 p.m., and the pulled-pork BBQ dinner will be served at 6:30. The event will feature a silent auction,* *50/50 Raffle*,* Cash Bar, Museum Tours and interactive entertainment by Jake Earles. The cost is $50/person or $350/Table of 8.* *To reserve your spot, call 276-403-5361, visit the museum or visit [https://www.mhchistoricalsociety.org/products-store?rq=blue%20jeans%20.](https://www.mhchistoricalsociety.org/products-store?rq=blue%20jeans%20)
