** 100 Years ago - 1925**

“NOTICE: Why send the hat away when it can be cleaned and blocked at home. LET ELAM DO IT with Davis Dry Cleaning Co., Phone 238.”

**75 years ago - 1950 **

Ridgeway Future Farmers of America members Conrad Knight, Alvin Scott and Kenneth Dunn attended the 24th State convention of FFA at V.P.I. in Blacksburg. They participated in a dairy judging contest the first night, then went on field trips for three days.

**50 years ago - 1975**

Glenn Richard Compton, 22, of Route 6, Martinsville, was killed after a wreck in which he had been driving a motorcycle which was hit by a van.

Across Martinsville and Henry County, 2,340 students were enrolled in summer school. Some of them were repeating classes they had failed, while some high school students were getting a jump on classes that would have been required in the year ahead.

**25 years ago - 2000**

Lillian Foster retired after 24 years as the crossing guard at Albert Harris Elementary School.
