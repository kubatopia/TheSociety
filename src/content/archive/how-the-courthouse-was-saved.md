The Henry County courthouse was built in 1824 and significantly expanded in
1929. It served the county for the better part of two centuries before it was
retired from use in 1996.

That is the year the Martinsville-Henry County Historical Society was formed:
not as a club, but as a response to a specific building with an uncertain
future. The Society took it on, and the courthouse became the MHC Heritage
Center & Museum.

The grounds are now a Garden Club of Virginia historic restoration site, and
the courtroom upstairs is open to visitors — left much as it was when the
county moved out.
