Sounds on the Square runs through the summer on the courthouse lawn, and has
become one of the Society's best-attended programmes. It closed out the 2026
season on Saturday 19 September with the Rosebud Band of Floyd.

The series leans on the music the region actually produced - string bands, old
time and bluegrass, and the players who kept those traditions going in Henry
County.
