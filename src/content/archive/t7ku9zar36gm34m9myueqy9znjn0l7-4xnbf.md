**By Holly Kozelsky and Pat Pion**

**100 Years ago – 1924**

At a Saturday meeting of the Henry County organization of the Tobacco Growers Association at the courthouse, the following representatives were elected: P.H. Reed, A.C. Minter, C.S. Turner, J.F. Wilson, R.W. Jones, W.H. Carter, S.H. Hooker and G.L. Mitchell.

**75 years ago – 1949**

This Ad: “6 cents – Get Twice As Much in Pepsi’s BIG Bottle! Pepsi tastes just twice as good – goes just twice as far – gives you twice as much! Pepsi Bottling Co. of Danville, Va.” The illustration shows a 12-ounce bottle which includes the text “2 Full Glasses of Pepsi.”

**1960**

The Fine Arts contest of the Virginia Federation of Women’s Clubs was hosted at Andrew Lewis High School in Salem. Mrs. Barbara Zimmerman, representing the Martinsville-Henry County Women’s Club, won first place in woodworking and first and second place in  the embroidering division, as well as being runner-up for first place in the Vogue Sewing Contest.

**50 years ago – 1974**

Salaries for Henry County teachers with no experience:1972-73, $7,000 and 1973-74, $7,200; with 18 years experience, $9,956 in 1972/73 and $10,504 in 1973/74. The School Board was requesting increases in salary to range from $500 per year for new teachers to $604 for those more experienced.

**25 years ago - 1999**

More than 1,000 people turned out for the Sportsman Club’s Eighth Annual Easter Egg Hunt and Cookout. Bruce Dallas was a club member and a Martinsville councilman. Casey Porter’s 16-month-old daughter, CaDasha Porter, was one of the raffle prize winners.
